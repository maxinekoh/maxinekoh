## Hi, I’m Maxine

I studied business before starting my MSc in Blockchain at NTU. Lately I’ve been curious about what happens when a DeFi system has to make a difficult call, like closing one side of a hedge. I like working through the details, writing them down, and building a small model or app when it helps me understand the problem.

I’ve also worked on cross-chain tracing. One question I keep coming back to is what you’d need to see before you could trust a match between two transactions on different chains.

### A couple of projects

- [ADL Hedge Analysis](https://github.com/maxinekoh/adl-hedge-analysis) pairs my paper for Blockchain @ NTU with a reading guide and a Python notebook that looks at how much of a disrupted hedge you’d need to put back.
- [FairHold](https://github.com/maxinekoh/SC6113-Individual-DApps) is a small escrow app I built on Ethereum’s Sepolia testnet to try out how payment releases and refunds work.
